Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 • Developer: Shivansh Verma • Contact: Shivanshverma123@gmail.com

Summary

MeterSplit stores your data on your phone and, if you sign in with Google, in a private backup folder in your own Google Drive. The developer has no servers for this app, does not receive, collect or view your data, does not sell or share it, and shows no ads.

Information the app handles

  • Google account details – your name and email address, received when you sign in with Google. They are used to show your account in the app and to make sure the Drive backup belongs to the same account.
  • Google Drive access – the app requests only the drive.appdata permission, which gives access to a hidden application-data folder created for this app. It cannot read, list or change any other file in your Drive. The app stores one file there, metersplit-history.json.
  • Data you enter – apartment and flat names, optional flat phone numbers, meter readings, bill amounts and optional rent amounts. This data is saved on your device and included in the backup file in your Drive.
  • MPIN – your 4-digit MPIN is kept in your device's secure storage and never leaves your phone.

How the information is used

Only to run the features you see in the app: calculating and showing your bills, backing up your data to your Drive, and restoring it when you sign in again. When you tap the WhatsApp button, the app opens WhatsApp with a prepared message; nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

Sharing, storage and security

The developer does not receive your data and does not share it with anyone. Your backup lives only in your own Google Drive and is transferred over encrypted (HTTPS) connections directly between your phone and Google. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking services.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MeterSplit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing features described above, is not transferred to others, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your phone.
  • To stop the app accessing your Drive, remove MeterSplit at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete the backup file, open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → MeterSplit → Delete hidden app data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
