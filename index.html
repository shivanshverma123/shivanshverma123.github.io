MeterSplit

Split one apartment electricity bill fairly across flats.

What it does

When an apartment has a single main electricity meter and each flat has its own sub-meter, MeterSplit works out each flat's share of the bill: it divides the total bill by the main meter's units to get a per-unit rate, then multiplies it by each flat's units. You can then send each flat its bill, and optionally a rent request, on WhatsApp.

Features

  • Unlimited apartments and flats, with each month's readings kept on your phone
  • Works fully offline, protected by a 4-digit MPIN
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive so your data can be restored on a new phone
  • Dark mode, and bills sent through WhatsApp

How the app uses your Google account

You sign in with Google to identify your account and to back up your data to a private, hidden application folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). The app cannot see your other Drive files, and the developer never receives your data. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
